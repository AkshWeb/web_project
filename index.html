The library

Twelve movements that cover a whole week of training.

Barbell Back Squat

  • Intermediate
  • Barbell
  • Legs · Core

The reference lower-body lift. Builds quads, glutes and the bracing strength everything else borrows from.

Push-Up

  • Beginner
  • Bodyweight
  • Chest · Arms

A moving plank. Trains the chest and triceps while teaching you to hold a rigid body under load.

Conventional Deadlift

  • Advanced
  • Barbell
  • Back · Legs

Picking heavy things up correctly. Works nearly every muscle on the back of your body at once.

Pull-Up

  • Advanced
  • Pull-up bar
  • Back · Arms

The clearest measure of upper-body pulling strength. Builds the lats, mid-back and biceps together.

Standing Overhead Press

  • Intermediate
  • Barbell
  • Shoulders · Arms

Pressing weight over your head while standing tall. Honest shoulder strength with nothing to hide behind.

Bent-Over Row

  • Intermediate
  • Barbell
  • Back · Arms

The balance to all your pressing. Thickens the mid-back and teaches you to hold a hinge position under load.

Dumbbell Bench Press

  • Beginner
  • Dumbbells
  • Chest · Arms

Kinder on the shoulders than a barbell, and each arm has to earn its own rep.

Walking Lunge

  • Beginner
  • Dumbbells
  • Legs · Core

One leg at a time, which exposes the side that has been coasting. Also brutal for balance.

Romanian Deadlift

  • Intermediate
  • Barbell
  • Legs · Back

The hamstring and glute builder. Teaches the hip hinge better than any other lift.

Front Plank

  • Beginner
  • Bodyweight
  • Core

Not about how long you can last. It is about how hard you can brace for a short time.

Glute Bridge

  • Beginner
  • Bodyweight
  • Legs · Core

Wakes up glutes that have spent the day in a chair. Useful before squats and deadlifts.

Farmer's Carry

  • Beginner
  • Dumbbells
  • Core · Grip

Pick up something heavy and walk. The most directly useful thing in this library.
