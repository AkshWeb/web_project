Learn to Exercise the Right Way

A simple library of exercises with muscle group, level and short instructions.

Barbell Back Squat

Legs Intermediate Barbell

Stand with feet shoulder width apart, bar on your shoulders. Bend your knees and lower down like sitting on a chair, then push back up.

Push-Up

Chest Beginner Bodyweight

Keep your body straight from head to heels. Lower your chest to the floor and push back up.

Conventional Deadlift

Back Advanced Barbell

Bend at the hips, grip the bar, keep your back flat and stand up straight while holding the bar.

Pull-Up

Back Advanced Pull-up Bar

Hang from the bar and pull yourself up until your chin is above it, then lower down slowly.

Overhead Press

Shoulders Intermediate Barbell

Hold the bar at your shoulders and press it straight up above your head, then lower back down.

Bent-Over Row

Back Intermediate Barbell

Bend forward slightly, let the bar hang, then pull it up towards your stomach and lower it back down.

Dumbbell Bench Press

Chest Beginner Dumbbells

Lie on a bench holding a dumbbell in each hand. Push them up above your chest, then lower slowly.

Walking Lunge

Legs Beginner Dumbbells

Step forward and lower your back knee towards the floor, then push up and step forward with the other leg.

Romanian Deadlift

Legs Intermediate Barbell

Keep your legs almost straight and lower the bar down your legs, then squeeze your hips forward to stand.

Front Plank

Core Beginner Bodyweight

Rest on your forearms and toes, keep your body in a straight line, and hold the position.

Glute Bridge

Legs Beginner Bodyweight

Lie on your back with knees bent, push your hips up towards the ceiling, then lower back down.

Farmer's Carry

Core Beginner Dumbbells

Hold a heavy dumbbell in each hand and walk in a straight line while keeping your body upright.
