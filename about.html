About FitFlex

Why we made this website and who made it.

Our Story

FitFlex was made in 2026 as a simple website to help people learn exercises the right way. Instead of guessing how to do a workout, users can look up an exercise and read short, clear instructions.

What We Believe

  • Instructions should be short and easy to follow.
  • Every exercise should show which muscles it works.
  • Beginners should have an easier version to start with.
